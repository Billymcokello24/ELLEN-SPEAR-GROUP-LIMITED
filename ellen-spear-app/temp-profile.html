Who We Are

1. Introduction & About Us

Ellen Spear Group is an industry-leading security and technology firm providing comprehensive physical guarding, intelligent surveillance, and cutting-edge ICT infrastructure for leading institutions across East Africa.

We engineer impenetrable security architectures by combining elite ground-force protection with advanced cybersecurity intelligence. We recognize that modern threats do not distinguish between the physical and digital realms, and neither does our approach.

With a 24/7 Command Centre, highly vetted personnel, and internationally certified operations, we deliver bespoke, mission-critical security tailored to the precise risk profiles of our clients.

Our operations span across multiple sectors including Corporate & Commercial, Government Institutions, Residential Estates, Industrial Manufacturing, and Financial Services. We deploy a hybrid model that ensures rapid response units are always backed by real-time tactical intelligence and robust network defense systems.

At Ellen Spear Group, we do not just react to threats; we anticipate and neutralize them before they materialize.

What We Do

5. Our Services

1. IT Security

In an era where digital threats evolve by the hour, Ellen Spear Group delivers enterprise-grade cybersecurity solutions engineered to shield your most critical data assets. Our IT Security division conducts comprehensive cybersecurity audits and risk assessments to uncover vulnerabilities before malicious actors can exploit them.

We deploy advanced network monitoring and intrusion prevention systems that operate around the clock, providing real-time visibility into your entire infrastructure. Our services also encompass robust data protection and backup solutions, endpoint and cloud security management, and tailored security awareness training programmes to ensure every member of your organization becomes a line of defense.

2. Physical Security Services

Ellen Spear Group provides a full spectrum of physical security services through rigorously trained, highly vetted personnel. Our guards maintain a commanding on-site presence across corporate offices, residential estates, industrial facilities, and institutional campuses, delivering access control and perimeter security that deters threats before they materialize.

We operate mobile patrol units that cover wide operational zones including businesses, estates, and government institutions. For high-profile clients, we offer dedicated VIP and executive protection services, ensuring personal safety with discretion, discipline, and tactical precision at every level.

What We Do

5. Our Services (continued)

3. Alarm & Rapid Response

Our state-of-the-art Command Centre operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, providing continuous monitoring of alarm systems installed across client premises. When an alert is triggered, our strategically positioned rapid response units are dispatched immediately, ensuring crisis containment within minutes of detection.

We specialize in the professional installation and monitoring of alarm systems, seamless CCTV integration with alarm alerts for visual verification, and comprehensive incident reporting and resolution. Every event is logged, analyzed, and followed up to ensure continuous improvement in your security posture.

4. Security Consultancy

Ellen Spear Group's consultancy division provides expert advisory services for organizations seeking to strengthen their overall security framework. We conduct thorough security risk and threat assessments that map your unique vulnerabilities, and then design and implement integrated security systems tailored precisely to your operational environment.

Our consultants deliver compliance and regulatory guidance to ensure your operations meet all industry and government security standards. We also develop crisis management and contingency planning strategies, preparing your organization to respond decisively and effectively to any emergency scenario.
